My dissertation investigates whether certain psychological traits influence susceptibility to AI manipulation in the context of social engineering attacks. I'm also a co‑author and research intern at the RC-Trust Human Understanding of Algorithms and Machines Lab, where I co-lead empirical studies on AI in psychotherapy and AI companions. My broader interests sit at the intersection of cognitive science, human-AI interaction, philosophy of AI, and the psychosocial effects of AI on individuals and society.
